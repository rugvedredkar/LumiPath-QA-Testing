# Safety Memo, Study ZX-204 (FICTIONAL, FOR SOFTWARE TESTING)

**Date:** 3 March 2026

## Summary
The independent data monitoring committee reviewed safety data after the first 60 participants completed Week 4.

## Findings
- Most common adverse events: headache (11%), nasopharyngitis (9%) and nausea (6%).
- Two participants had ALT above 3 times the upper limit of normal; both resolved after dose interruption.
- No deaths and no cases of venous thromboembolism were reported.

## Recommendation
Continue the study without modification. Repeat the review after 120 participants complete Week 4.
